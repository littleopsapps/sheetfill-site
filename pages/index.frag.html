Your spreadsheet stays on your computer

There is no server. SheetFill has no account, no sign-in and nothing to sign up for. What you paste is stored in your own browser and is never uploaded. The only thing SheetFill ever sends over the internet is a license key, to check that it is valid.

Password fields are excluded in code and can never be filled. You can erase everything SheetFill holds with one click in Settings.

What SheetFill deliberately will not do

These are permanent limits, not missing features. They are what keep you in control of every record you create, and what keep SheetFill a tool rather than a bot.

  • It never submits a form. SheetFill fills the fields. You look at them and press submit yourself — every row, every time.
  • It does not handle CAPTCHAs. There is no CAPTCHA solving, no OTP handling, and no attempt to work around any anti-automation measure. There never will be.
  • It does not run unattended bulk automation. There is no mode that loops through your rows on its own. Each row needs a separate click from you.

Questions

Which browsers does it work in?

Microsoft Edge and Firefox. It is built on standard Manifest V3, so it runs anywhere that does.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. If you can click a field and pick an item from a list, you can use it. There are no selectors, scripts, macros or settings to configure.

What happens if the website changes?

SheetFill identifies fields by several signals at once, so small changes usually do not break anything. If a field genuinely cannot be found, SheetFill names it and asks you to re-teach it — it never quietly skips a field or guesses.

Can it fill dropdowns?

Yes — matched on the visible text, so “United Kingdom” in your spreadsheet finds “United Kingdom” in the list. Checkboxes understand Yes/No, True/False, 1/0 and X.

What if I map a column wrong?

Click “Undo last fill”. The previous values come back and the row counter steps back. Then remove the wrong mapping and teach it again.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. There is no server. See the privacy policy.

Can it submit the forms too?

No, by design. See above.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Yes. Because nothing syncs, you paste your spreadsheet and teach your forms separately on each machine.
