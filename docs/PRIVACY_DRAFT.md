## The short version

SheetFill runs entirely on your computer. Your spreadsheet, your field mappings and your progress
are stored in your own browser and are never uploaded. There is no account, no sign-in and no
server that holds your data.

We do not publish a postal address here. If you need our full business contact details — for a legal notice, or any other reason — email littleops.apps@gmail.com and we will provide them without delay.

## What SheetFill stores on your computer

- The spreadsheet rows you paste
- Which form field is fed by which column, per website
- Which row you are on
- How many rows you have filled this month
- Your license key, if you have bought Pro

All of it is held in your browser's local extension storage. You can erase all of it at any time
from **Settings → Delete everything**, or by uninstalling the extension.

## What SheetFill sends over the internet

One thing: if you enter a license key, that key is sent to our payment provider, Lemon Squeezy, to
check that it is valid. Nothing else is ever transmitted — not your spreadsheet, not the pages you
visit, not what you fill in.

## What SheetFill never touches

- Passwords. Password fields are excluded from filling entirely.
- The pages you browse, their content, or your history.
- Any usage analytics. We do not operate analytics of any kind.

## Purchases

Payments are handled by **Lemon Squeezy**, who act as Merchant of Record and are the seller of
record for your purchase. They collect the billing information needed to process payment and tax.
We never see or store your card details. Their privacy policy governs that data:
https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/privacy

## Cookies

The SheetFill extension uses no cookies. The website uses no cookies and no analytics.

## Your rights

Because your data never leaves your computer, we hold nothing about you to access, export or
delete. You are in sole possession of it, and **Settings → Delete everything** removes it.

For purchase records, contact Lemon Squeezy or write to littleops.apps@gmail.com and we will help.

SheetFill is a workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13.
